# Vol2day Event Check-In Process

## Project Overview

**Product:** Vol2day Volunteer Management Platform  
**Document Type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Audience:** Event coordinators and volunteer check-in staff  

**Skills Demonstrated:**
- Internal documentation
- Process documentation
- Workflow design
- Operational clarity

---

# Purpose

This procedure outlines the standard process for checking volunteers in at events using Vol2day.

Following this process helps organizations maintain accurate attendance records and provide volunteers with a smooth arrival experience.

---

# Scope

This procedure applies to staff members responsible for volunteer check-in during scheduled events.

---

# Responsibilities

## Event Coordinator

The event coordinator is responsible for:

- Confirming event details before arrival
- Ensuring staff have access to check-in tools
- Reviewing attendance records after the event

## Check-In Staff

Check-in staff are responsible for:

- Greeting volunteers
- Verifying volunteer information
- Recording attendance accurately
- Escalating issues when needed

---

# Procedure

## Before the Event

1. Sign in to Vol2day.

2. Open the scheduled event.

3. Confirm:
   - Event date and time
   - Volunteer list
   - Assigned staff access

4. Prepare check-in equipment.

---

## During Volunteer Arrival

1. Welcome the volunteer.

2. Search for the volunteer's name in Vol2day.

3. Confirm the volunteer's information.

4. Select **Check In**.

5. Verify that attendance has been recorded successfully.

---

## Handling Common Issues

### Volunteer Does Not Appear on the List

Confirm:

- The volunteer registered for the event.
- The event details are correct.
- The volunteer is assigned to the correct opportunity.

If the issue continues, contact an administrator.

---

### Duplicate Check-In Attempt

Verify the volunteer's attendance status before making changes.

Do not create duplicate attendance records.

---

# After the Event

1. Review attendance records.

2. Confirm all volunteer hours are accurate.

3. Report any discrepancies to the organization administrator.

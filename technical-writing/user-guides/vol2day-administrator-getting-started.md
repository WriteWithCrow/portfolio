# Welcome to Vol2day

Vol2day helps organizations recruit, manage, and engage volunteers through one centralized platform.

This guide introduces administrators to the core tools needed to set up their organization, create volunteer opportunities, and manage engagement activities.

---

# Getting Started

## Step 1: Set Up Your Organization Profile

Your organization profile helps volunteers understand who you are and what your mission represents.

Complete the following information:

- Organization name
- Description
- Logo or branding
- Contact information
- Website and social media links

A complete profile helps build trust and encourages volunteer participation.

---

## Step 2: Create Your First Volunteer Opportunity

Volunteer opportunities allow organizations to share available roles and invite community members to participate.

When creating an opportunity, include:

- Opportunity title
- Description
- Date and time
- Location
- Volunteer requirements
- Instructions for volunteers

Clear opportunities help volunteers determine whether a role is a good fit before registering.

---

## Step 3: Manage Volunteer Registrations

Once volunteers begin registering, administrators can:

- Review volunteer information
- Confirm participation
- Communicate updates
- Track attendance

---

## Step 4: Track Volunteer Engagement

Vol2day provides tools to help organizations understand their volunteer impact.

Administrators can use reporting tools to review:

- Volunteer hours
- Participation trends
- Event attendance
- Engagement history

---

# Best Practices

## Keep Information Updated

Regularly review organization details and volunteer opportunities to ensure information remains accurate.

## Use Clear Descriptions

Volunteers are more likely to participate when they understand:

- What they will do
- What is expected
- How their contribution helps

## Recognize Volunteer Contributions

Acknowledging volunteer efforts helps build long-term engagement and stronger community relationships.

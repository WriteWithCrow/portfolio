## July 2026 Product Update

We're excited to share the latest improvements to Vol2day! This release includes new tools designed to make volunteer coordination easier, improve staff workflows, and help organizations create better experiences for their communities.

---

## New Feature: Staff Mode

### Manage volunteer check-ins with confidence

Staff members can now access a dedicated Staff Mode from the Check-In menu, providing a streamlined experience for managing volunteer arrivals and departures.

With Staff Mode, organizations can:

- Quickly locate scheduled volunteers
- Check volunteers in and out of events
- Reduce wait times during busy events
- Provide staff with a focused check-in experience

Staff Mode was designed with busy event teams in mind, helping coordinators spend less time managing logistics and more time engaging with their volunteers.

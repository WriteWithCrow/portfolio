# Welcome to Vol2day

This guide helps staff members learn the essential tools needed to support volunteer events using Vol2day.

By the end of this training, staff members will be able to:

- Access volunteer event information
- Check volunteers in and out
- Update attendance records
- Resolve common check-in issues

---

# Module 1: Understanding Your Role

Staff members help create a positive volunteer experience by:

- Welcoming volunteers
- Providing accurate information
- Recording attendance
- Escalating issues when needed

A smooth check-in experience helps volunteers feel prepared and valued.

---

# Module 2: Accessing an Event

Before volunteers arrive:

1. Sign in to Vol2day.

2. Navigate to your assigned event.

3. Review:
   - Event details
   - Volunteer list
   - Staff assignments

Confirm that all information is accurate before check-in begins.

---

# Module 3: Checking In Volunteers

When a volunteer arrives:

1. Welcome the volunteer.

2. Search for their name.

3. Confirm their information.

4. Select **Check In**.

5. Verify that attendance has been recorded.

---

# Module 4: Troubleshooting

## A volunteer cannot be found

Check:

- The volunteer registered for the event.
- The event is active.
- The volunteer is assigned correctly.

If the issue continues, contact an administrator.

---

## Attendance was recorded incorrectly

Do not create a second attendance record.

Notify an administrator so the record can be corrected.

---

# Knowledge Check

Before completing training, staff should be able to answer:

1. Where do you find assigned events?

2. What steps should you follow when checking in a volunteer?

3. Who should you contact if a problem cannot be resolved?

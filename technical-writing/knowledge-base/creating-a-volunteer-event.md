## Overview

Vol2day makes it easy for organizations to create and manage volunteer opportunities. This guide walks administrators through creating a new volunteer event and publishing it for volunteers to view.

---

## Before You Begin

Before creating an event, make sure you have:

- Administrator access to your organization account
- Event details, including date, time, and location
- Volunteer requirements and instructions

---

## Create a Volunteer Event

1. Sign in to your Vol2day account.

2. From the dashboard, select **Opportunities**.

3. Click **Create New Opportunity**.

4. Enter the event details:
   - Opportunity title
   - Description
   - Date and time
   - Location
   - Required volunteer roles

5. Add any additional information volunteers need before signing up.

6. Review your opportunity details, then select **Publish**.

Your volunteer opportunity is now available for volunteers to view and register.

---

## Tips for Creating Effective Opportunities

Clear, detailed opportunities help volunteers understand what to expect.

Consider including:

- A brief description of the organization's mission
- Expected duties
- Required skills or experience
- Arrival instructions
- Contact information for questions

---

## Troubleshooting

### Why can't I publish my opportunity?

Make sure all required fields are completed. Required information is marked with an asterisk (*).

### Why can't volunteers see my opportunity?

Confirm that the opportunity status is set to **Published** and that registration dates are active.

# About the Author

Brittany Crow writes character-driven speculative fiction exploring love, fear, identity, and the boundaries between the ordinary and the impossible.

Her stories blend elements of supernatural fiction, fantasy, psychological horror, and romance, focusing on characters forced to confront hidden truths, impossible choices, and the parts of themselves they cannot escape.

When she isn't writing, Brittany enjoys exploring stories that examine human resilience, complicated relationships, and what happens when ordinary people encounter extraordinary circumstances.

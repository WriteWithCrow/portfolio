## Project Overview

**Genre:** Medical Drama Romance

**Target Audience:** Adult contemporary romance readers  

**Skills Demonstrated:**
- Character development
- Emotional pacing
- Dialogue
- Scene construction
- Balancing professional and personal stakes

---

## About the Story

Dr. Elena Marlowe has spent years building a reputation as one of the most disciplined surgeons at St. Catherine's Medical Center. Control, precision, and perfection are the only things standing between her and the life she's worked for.

Then comes the one mistake she can't explain.

Assigned to work alongside ICU nurse Jackson Reid during the investigation that threatens both their careers, Elena discovers the one thing more dangerous than losing control: letting someone see the person beneath the armor.
